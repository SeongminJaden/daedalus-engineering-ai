# A six axis manipulator, through every stage this project has

This document is generated by `scripts/design_manipulator.py`, which runs the
whole pipeline on one arm and writes the tables below. Re-run it with

    .venv/bin/python scripts/design_manipulator.py --out data/generated/manipulator_v1

The geometry it emits (STEP files from the family search, STL from the free
form path, URDF and SDF for the assembly) lands under that output directory,
which is ignored by git like every other generated artefact; what is committed
is this document and the code that produced it.

The specification lives in `projects/manipulator/spec.py`, where every number
says whether it was GIVEN by the task or CHOSEN here, and why.

Everything here is SIMULATED or a rule set. No part has been made and no number has been measured on hardware. The stages that could not run say so; that list is half the result.

## What this design can and cannot claim

**It can claim** that a six axis arm of this geometry, with these sections, holds the rated payload at full reach with the stated safety factor and deflection limit ACCORDING TO this project's solvers, and that two independent engines agree with its rigid body dynamics to twelve digits and with its statics to 0.03 percent.

**It cannot claim** that the arm works. Nothing has been built. Two joints have no drive at all, so as a machine it is incomplete, and the reason they have none is that the catalogue pages do not print the values the selection needs. The link masses come from a sizing routine that solves for one dimension of three. The free form parts are solved once with linear tetrahedra and their deflections are lower bounds. Friction, backlash and joint compliance are all zero because no measured parameters exist for them, so every torque here is a lower bound too. No cover, no wiring, no bearings beyond a seat tolerance, and no bolted joint analysis.

**The evidence grade of every number in this document is SIMULATED or below**, except the manufacturability rows, which are a rule set with its own grade and are not evidence at all.
